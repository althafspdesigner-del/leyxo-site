# leyxo.com

Coming-soon waitlist page for Leyxo Studio, served by GitHub Pages at https://leyxo.com. Dark Ink theme, with a 3D gallery of 15 cards that fans out on load, spins continuously and speeds up when you scroll.

- `index.html`: the whole page (English and Arabic, language toggle, notify-me form that opens an email to hello@leyxo.com)
- `assets/cards/`: the 15 gallery images (520 × 520 JPEG)
- `assets/`: logos, photo, favicon set, social share image, self-hosted Geist, Geist Mono and Readex Pro fonts (SIL Open Font Licence)
- `CNAME`: the custom domain
